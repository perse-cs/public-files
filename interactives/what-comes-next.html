    Is this how AI writes?

    AI text generators build text one small piece at a time. Those pieces are called tokens: they can be whole words, parts of words, or punctuation. A model uses the text so far to predict possible next tokens.

    Here, you make the choices. Our demo follows a word map written by people, with no AI model running. Every button is one possible next word, and all buttons are shown equally — they are not probability scores.
